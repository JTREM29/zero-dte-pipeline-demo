# REST
## Partners

### Economic Calendar

**Endpoint:** `GET /benzinga/v1/economic-calendar`

**Description:**

A comprehensive database of economic calendar events including key economic indicators, central bank meetings, and government reports with actual values, consensus forecasts, and prior data for economic analysis and market forecasting. Events are categorized by importance level and include detailed descriptions explaining their significance.

Use Cases: Economic event tracking, market forecasting, trading signals, macroeconomic analysis, portfolio risk management.
