# REST
## Partners

### Government Trades

**Endpoint:** `GET /benzinga/v1/government-trades`

**Description:**

A comprehensive database of financial trades and transactions made by members of the United States Congress and other government officials, including stocks, bonds, and other securities, with detailed information about the trading member, security details, and transaction specifics.

Use Cases: Congressional trading analysis, political investment tracking, transparency research, compliance monitoring.
